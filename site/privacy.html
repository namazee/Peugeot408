Privacy policy

DashCraft · last updated 21 November 2026

In short: DashCraft does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives what you enter.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter or the app creates — service and fuel records, renewals, Touch ’n Go balance, parking spots, home and work places, photos you pick, slot designs and sounds — is stored only on your iPhone, in a container shared between the app and its widgets. Backups exist only if you export one yourself, and they go wherever you choose to save them.

What leaves your iPhone, only to answer a request

  • Weather: your approximate location is sent to Apple WeatherKit to get the forecast.
  • Air quality: only if you add your own WAQI key, your location is sent to the World Air Quality Index service.
  • Prayer times: the JAKIM zone code (not your location) is sent to JAKIM’s e-Solat service.
  • Drive times and maps: Apple Maps (MapKit) is used for routes and map pictures.

These services answer the request; DashCraft does not send them anything else and does not keep a copy elsewhere.

Permissions

  • Location — saving where you parked, finding your prayer zone, local weather and drive times.
  • Calendar — showing your next event on the Commute widget.
  • Bluetooth — connecting to an OBD2 adapter, if you use one.
  • Apple Music — showing what you play on the Music widget.
  • Photos — only the pictures you pick are copied into the app.

Every permission is optional; you can change it any time in iPhone Settings.

Children

DashCraft is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at GitHub.
