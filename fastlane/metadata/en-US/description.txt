Make the CarPlay screen yours. DashCraft gives you five Car Slot widgets that you design on your iPhone; the car screen updates by itself.

YOUR CAR AT A GLANCE
• Service due by km or date, whichever comes first
• Fuel log with km/L, price per litre and monthly spend
• Road tax and insurance renewal countdowns
• Toll card balance with a low-balance warning
• Live OBD2 values (boost, coolant, battery, RPM) with an optional Bluetooth adapter

YOUR DAY ON THE DASHBOARD
• Prayer times with the next prayer counting down (JAKIM zones, Malaysia)
• Weather and haze, drive time to home or work, your next calendar event
• Where you parked, with a map picture
• A photo frame for family pictures, and your Apple Music

DESIGN IT YOUR WAY
• Split each slot into one, two or four cards
• Bold and Neon looks for every card, any background colour
• Template gallery, custom builder and your own car photo in the middle
• Panorama: one wide picture across three columns, with a greeting and the clock
• Seasonal packs for Hari Raya, Chinese New Year, Deepavali, Merdeka, Christmas and more

SOUNDS
• Engine starts, EV hums, luxury chimes and more when CarPlay connects, plus a disconnect sound and a spoken greeting (set up once with a Shortcuts automation)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone, and you can back it up to one file.

Try everything with Settings → Sample data before you enter your own.

Needs iOS 26 or later and a car with CarPlay for the car screen; the widgets also work on the iPhone. Not affiliated with any car maker.
