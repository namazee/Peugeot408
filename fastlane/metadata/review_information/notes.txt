No login is needed.

To see every widget filled without a car or your own data: open Settings → Sample data and turn it on (or tap "Try it with sample data" on the last onboarding page). A "Sample data" badge shows while it is on.

CarPlay: on iOS 26, any iPhone widget can be added to the CarPlay widget screen without a CarPlay entitlement (Settings → General → CarPlay → [car] → Widgets → add "Car Slot 1" … "Car Slot 5"). The same widgets also work on the iPhone Home Screen for review. The app plays no audio in CarPlay itself and has no navigation.

Connect sounds play through an App Intent that the user adds to a Shortcuts automation ("When CarPlay connects → Play Connect Sound").

Live OBD2 values need an optional Bluetooth LE OBD2 adapter; Car → OBD2 has a demo mode that shows made-up values without one.

Prayer times, toll card and road tax renewals are Malaysia-specific features.
